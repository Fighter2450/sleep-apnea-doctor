Sleep Apnea Dental Specialists

A network of board-certified dental sleep medicine practices helping patients breathe better and sleep well — without surgery.

What connects these practices?

Every practice on this page shares the same core approach: treating obstructive sleep apnea through oral appliance therapy — a custom-fitted mouthguard worn at night that keeps the airway open. All providers hold board certifications in dental sleep medicine (D-ABDSM) and offer CPAP alternatives. Several also treat TMJ disorders and craniofacial pain, conditions closely linked to sleep-disordered breathing. Together they represent a growing field where dentists and sleep specialists collaborate to give patients comfortable, effective options beyond the traditional CPAP machine.
